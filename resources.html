NATIONAL LEGISLATION

Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021

Republic of Zambia · 2021

Establishes the legal framework for the protection of personal data in Zambia, including obligations relevant to organisations processing personal data.

About this legislation

Relevant areas include data controller and processor obligations, security safeguards, data protection impact assessments, personal data breaches and automated decision-making.

View legislation at Parliament ↗
NATIONAL LEGISLATION

Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

Republic of South Africa · 2013

Regulates the processing of personal information and establishes conditions for lawful processing and protection of personal information.

About this legislation

Relevant provisions include lawful processing, security safeguards, notification of security compromises, automated decision-making and cross-border transfers of personal information.

View official government resource ↗

PRACTICAL ORGANISATIONAL GUIDE

AI Governance Readiness Checklist

African AI Governance Hub · Practical guide

A starting-point checklist for organisations reviewing their readiness to adopt or use AI responsibly.

Explore the checklist
  • Identify AI systems currently in use or planned.
  • Assign responsibility for AI oversight.
  • Review personal data and privacy implications.
  • Identify potential bias and discrimination risks.
  • Establish human review and escalation procedures.
  • Document AI-related risks and controls.
  • Review and update governance practices regularly.

Developed as general guidance by the Hub.

PRACTICAL ORGANISATIONAL GUIDE

AI Risk Review Guide

African AI Governance Hub · Practical guide

A simple guide to help organisations identify and document potential risks before introducing or changing an AI system.

Explore the review steps
  • Describe the AI system and its intended purpose.
  • Identify individuals and groups potentially affected.
  • Consider privacy, security and data quality risks.
  • Consider accuracy, bias and reliability risks.
  • Assess the consequences of incorrect outputs.
  • Identify human oversight and mitigation measures.
  • Record decisions, responsible persons and review dates.

Developed as general guidance by the Hub.

PRACTICAL ORGANISATIONAL GUIDE

AI Use Policy – Key Considerations

African AI Governance Hub · Policy guide

Key areas organisations may consider when developing an internal policy governing employee use of AI tools.

Explore policy considerations
  • Define the purpose and scope of permitted AI use.
  • Identify approved and prohibited AI tools.
  • Set rules for confidential and personal information.
  • Require human verification of AI-generated outputs.
  • Address intellectual property and attribution.
  • Assign oversight and reporting responsibilities.
  • Provide staff training and periodic policy reviews.

Developed as general guidance by the Hub.
